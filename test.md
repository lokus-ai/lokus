# A Poem

In lines of code that dance and flow,
Where algorithms come and go,
A developer sits with focused mind,
Seeking solutions they hope to find.

Variables declare their silent names,
Functions play their coding games,
While loops that spin and conditions test,
Each keystroke brings us closer to our quest.

Through debugging nights and feature days,
We craft our digital displays,
Building bridges from thought to screen,
Creating worlds that can't be seen.

So here's to code, both clean and bright,
That turns our visions into sight,
In this realm of zeros and ones,
Where every program's story runs.
