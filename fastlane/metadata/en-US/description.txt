Lokus is a local-first workspace for people who think in writing. Your notes are plain Markdown files in a folder you control — no lock-in, no cloud requirement, no telemetry.

WRITE WITHOUT FRICTION
• A fast, beautiful Markdown editor with live formatting, tables, callouts, math (LaTeX), diagrams, and code blocks
• Wiki links between notes with instant autocomplete — build a knowledge base as you type
• Command palette and keyboard-first workflows throughout

SEE YOUR THINKING
• An interactive graph view maps every connection between your notes — in 2D or 3D
• Backlinks show every note that references the one you're reading
• Infinite canvas for freeform diagrams and visual thinking

ORGANIZE REAL WORK
• Kanban boards stored as plain files alongside your notes
• Tasks captured anywhere with checkbox syntax, tracked in one place
• Database views ("Bases") turn note frontmatter into sortable, filterable tables

YOUR CALENDAR, EVERYWHERE
• Connect multiple Google, iCloud, Outlook, and Notion accounts plus iCal feeds
• Every event syncs into a local database — the calendar opens instantly and works offline
• Create and edit events without leaving your notes; changes sync back automatically

PRIVATE BY ARCHITECTURE
• Everything lives on your device: notes, boards, calendar data, credentials in the system keychain
• Works fully offline
• No account required, no tracking

Your files. Your machine. Your second brain.
