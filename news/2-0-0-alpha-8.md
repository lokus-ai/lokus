---
title: Your team's notes arrive by themselves
summary: If you are in a team, its folder now appears and syncs on every computer you sign in on. Nothing to press.
version: 2.0.0-alpha.8
show: quiet
---
**New**

- **Teams sync by themselves.** Sign in and each team you are in gets its own folder in Documents, named after the team, and starts syncing. No more "Sync this vault here"
- Your own notes stay yours: a team never lands in a folder that already holds your files
- "Stop syncing" on a computer is remembered there

**Fixed (since alpha.6)**

- A connection that went quiet without saying so is noticed within a minute and opened again
- Two notes made apart under the same name are both kept, on every computer
- Identical notes are no longer doubled as "(local copy)" when a folder joins a team
- A computer that was offline no longer undoes a teammate's rename or brings back a deleted note
