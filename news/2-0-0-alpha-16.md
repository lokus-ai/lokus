version: 2.0.0-alpha.16
show: quiet

**Assistant**

- **Helpers.** For a big job, like going through every customer, the assistant starts up to four helpers that read at the same time and each bring back a short answer. Only the assistant itself changes your notes, and still asks first
- **Each helper has its own row in the chat:** what it is doing, for how long, and a stop button. Open a row to see its brief, steps and answer. Stop ends all of them
- **Ask a helper again.** A follow-up goes to the same helper, which keeps what it already read, also after a restart
- **Research mode.** Type /research, or ask for a deep look or an audit. The assistant writes a plan, gathers in waves of helpers, has a fresh helper check the claims against their sources, writes a report, then makes the changes
- Small questions are answered as before, with no helpers
- When several notes are read in one step, they are read at the same time

**Team sync**

- **A team folder shows its team.** The sidebar, the workspace list and the Team page show the team's name and how many members it has. Two folders of the same team are told apart by their folder names
- **The Team page says which account is signed in.** An account that is not a member of the folder's team is told so and offered "Use another account"; it can no longer share the folder as a new team
- **No second team with the same name.** Sharing is refused when you are already in a team of that name, with a button that opens that team
- **Joining a team never shares a private folder unasked.** A team you join gets its own folder; a folder holding your own notes is used only when you choose "In this folder"

**Fixed**

- On a relay of your own, the Team page shows the right person and member count
- A folder's access token is sent only to that folder's own relay
