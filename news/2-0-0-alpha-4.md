**New**

- **In the menu bar.** Closing the window or pressing ⌘Q keeps Lokus running there. Click the mark to open it, ask for a look now, or quit it for good
- A number beside the mark says how many things the assistant found that need you
- Turn it off under Settings, Assistant, "Keep Lokus in the menu bar"
