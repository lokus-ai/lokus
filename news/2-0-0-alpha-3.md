**New**

- **Invite by email.** Settings, Team, Invite people. They get an email, sign in with that address and join in one click
- **Workspaces.** Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar to switch, or to add one. One for the team, one for school, nothing shared between them
- **Pictures, PDFs and CSVs** open in Lokus, and can be attached to a message with +, a drop or a paste
- **The assistant makes files.** Ask for a chart, a spreadsheet or a PDF and it appears under the reply
- **Asks first or Auto.** A switch in the message box decides whether the assistant asks before each change

**Fixed**

- Command-W closes the tab, not the app
- Pages no longer overlap when the window is narrow
- Setup needs you to sign in, so every workspace has your account
