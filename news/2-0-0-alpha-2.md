**What changed**

- Setup asks you to **sign in**, with Google or an email and password
- Setup has a step to **turn the assistant on**, using the ChatGPT plan you already have or your own key
- If Lokus is opened straight from the download, it tells you to move it into **Applications** first
- The list of services to connect was empty in the first build. It is back

Nothing you had has moved.
