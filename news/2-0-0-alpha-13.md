title: Tasks you can edit on the board, and drafts that stay
summary: Change a task's assignee, status, priority and due date on the board, add your own columns, and see when an edit is saved. A chat message you were writing is still there when you come back. The Mac menu bar has the commands you look for there.
version: 2.0.0-alpha.13
show: quiet
---
**Tasks**

- **A task shows whether an edit is saved.** Unsaved, saving, saved or failed; a failed edit keeps what you typed and offers Retry
- **Edit on the board.** Assignee, status, priority and due date change in place, without opening the task
- **Your own columns.** Add, rename and remove columns on a board. An empty column stays
- **Delete a task** from the board. It goes to the trash like any note

**Assistant**

- **A message you were writing is kept,** with its attachments, when you close the chat, open another conversation or switch folder
- A suggestion from Noticed that finishes late stays in the conversation and folder it was started in
- Connecting the same provider again keeps the model you chose

**Menus**

- **The Mac menu bar carries Lokus's commands,** including Settings, and each goes to the window in front
- Closing Settings puts the cursor and selection back where they were, including inside a table cell

**Fixed**

- Renaming a file or folder while another folder is opening is refused instead of landing in the wrong place
- Your cursor and name are no longer sent to teammates in a different team folder during a switch
- An invitation approved in the browser while setup is asking about it is taken as joined, without another click
