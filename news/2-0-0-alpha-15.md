**Team sync**

- **A team folder stays in its team until you press Leave.** A lost connection, a sleeping computer or an expired sign-in no longer takes it out. It reconnects by itself as soon as there is internet
- **Lokus no longer signs you out by itself.** The sign-in is renewed quietly, also with the window hidden and with two copies of Lokus open. If it truly cannot be renewed, there is one "Sign in again", and you come back to the same folder and team
- **Lokus never switches workspace for you.** A team folder it finds is added to your list, not opened over the one you are in
- What you or the assistant changed while offline reaches your team once it reconnects
- The Team page was offering to share a stopped team folder again, and pressing Share made a second team with the same name. A folder in a team now only ever shows that team
- The team a folder belongs to is kept with the folder, so an update or a restart keeps it, and a folder you moved or renamed is still in its team when you open it
- The count under a team's name says how many notes are in the workspace; it used to say "shared", which it could not know

**Assistant**

- **Slash commands.** Type / in the chat box: /compact, /context and /clear
- **Compact a long conversation.** /compact writes a summary and carries on from it; add a note to say what to keep, like "/compact keep the pricing decisions". Near the limit it compacts by itself. Earlier messages stay, folded away
- **See how much context is used,** and what is using it: earlier messages, tool results, attachments
- **Read your iMessages (Mac).** Connect it under Connections; the assistant can read and search, never send. The background Noticed look does not read them
- The same conversation open in two windows no longer loses a message

**Fixed**

- **Sign in with ChatGPT stays signed in.** Lokus was signing you out by itself when two requests renewed the sign-in at the same moment. A busy moment at ChatGPT's sign-in service or a long answer no longer signs you out either
- **The assistant's box empties when you press Enter.** What you type while an answer is arriving is kept as your next message, and a message that was stopped or failed comes back to the box
- **One Lokus menu on right-click everywhere,** by mouse and keyboard, in place of the browser's own menu
- The Noticed list follows the workspace you switch to
