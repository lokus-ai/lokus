title: The assistant stays signed in, and the box empties when you send
summary: Sign in with ChatGPT no longer signs you out by itself. The assistant's box empties the moment you press Enter, every time, and a message that was not answered comes back to it.
version: 2.0.0-alpha.15
show: quiet
---
**New**

- **iMessage connects on a Mac.** In Settings, Connections, the assistant can read your recent conversations from Messages once you give Lokus Full Disk Access. It only reads: Lokus cannot send a message

**Fixed**

- **Sign in with ChatGPT stays signed in.** Lokus was signing you out by itself when two requests renewed the sign-in at the same moment. You do not need to sign in again
- A busy moment at ChatGPT's sign-in service no longer signs you out either; Lokus says to try again
- A long answer that outlasts its sign-in renews it and carries on, instead of signing you out
- Two copies of Lokus open on the same computer no longer sign each other out
- **The assistant's box empties when you press Enter.** Before, what you sent stayed in the box until the answer had finished, and stayed for good if you typed meanwhile
- What you type while an answer is arriving is kept as your next message
- A message that was stopped or failed comes back to the box, so you can send it again
- **A team folder whose sync stopped is reconnected to its team.** The Team page was offering to share it again, and pressing Share made a second team with the same name and nobody else in it. It now shows your team with a Reconnect button
- A folder in "Sync stopped" asks to connect again by itself whenever you come back to Lokus
- The count under a team's name says how many notes are in the workspace; it used to say "shared", which it could not know
